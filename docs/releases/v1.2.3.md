## Desktop assistant movement and collapse controls

- Let the AI icon and conversation join other applications' full-screen spaces and Stage Manager groups.
- Use native window dragging for the floating icon. Screen-edge correction happens after the drag ends, so crossing windows and displays is not restricted by the starting screen. The icon's position is saved.
- Give the collapse button a full 36 × 36 click target and accept the first click while Qingfeng is inactive. Drag the title area to move the conversation; toolbar buttons no longer compete with background window dragging. Collapse remains available during analysis.

Includes four focused AppKit checks covering screen placement, drag/click separation, collapse hit areas at three window widths, and collapse during analysis, alongside the existing 58 core and 8 HTTP checks.
