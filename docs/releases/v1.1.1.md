## Complete storage overview

- The homepage now divides the whole disk into **cleanup candidates**, **other used space**, and **available space**, with a capacity and percentage for each.
- The four cleanup category cards also show percentages of total disk capacity.
- Other used space is the remaining used capacity, including system files, installed apps, personal files, Trash, snapshots, and unscanned data. System and app sizes are not measured separately.
- Unscanned space is labeled as awaiting classification. Unknown capacity and inconsistent estimates are handled without negative values or invalid chart segments.

## Download and install

**[Download Qingfeng 1.1.1 for Apple Silicon](https://github.com/zealerFT/qingfeng/releases/download/v1.1.1/Qingfeng-1.1.1-AppleSilicon.zip)**

Quit Qingfeng, extract the ZIP, and drag **清风.app** into **Applications**, choosing **Replace** for an existing installation. No compilation is required. Requires macOS 14 or later and Apple Silicon, including M3. The app interface is Simplified Chinese.

The app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks a download you trust, try opening it once, then use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, if offered.

Files move to the system Trash. Review or restore them there before emptying Trash yourself to reclaim space.
